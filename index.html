Dancing with a Pirate

Historical Fantasy Romance Adventure Pirates

Liam and Rick are prisoners in the king of Hiverna's court. Destined to escape, and feel freedom once more. They will have to face their past in order to make a future for themselves. How far would you go to be free?

Ines a princess of Hiverna has seen nothing of the world. Trapped in her own cage she is aching for freedom. Will one night change her life forever? Will she be able to trust the ones she once held dear? Thrown into the world of pirates. Ines will have to learn who to trust, or fight for her life.

The Storyteller

Hope Beckett

Hope Beckett weaves tales of dark oceans, forgotten kingdoms, and loves forged in the fires of rebellion. Writing from her cabin in the Pacific Northwest, she spends her days fueled by copious amounts of black coffee, accompanied by her two rescue hounds, and drawing inspiration from the wild, untamed nature outside her window.
